Free League of Legends A–Z Challenge tracker

LoLphabet helps you win a game with every League of Legends champion, one by one. Pick a list (roles, races, classes, regions or your own custom list) and a challenge mode: Classic, Random, Hard, Random + Hard, Insanity or Random + Insanity.

Your wins, attempts, dates and notes are saved in your browser, or synced across devices when you log in with Google. The Next up box shows each champion's pick rate, win rate and a popular build when data is available. It is free, with no ads and no third-party analytics. Read more about LoLphabet.

About LoLphabet

LoLphabet is a free completion tracker for the League of Legends A–Z Challenge: win a game with every champion on a list, working through them one by one. It keeps your progress, attempts and notes in one place, so you can see how far you've come and what is left.

What you can track

  • Roles: Top, Jungle, Mid, Bot / ADC and Support, kept up to date from community-maintained champion data.
  • Races: Yordles, Vastayan, Darkin, Void creatures, Celestials and the Undead.
  • Classes: Fighter, Mage, Assassin, Tank, Marksman and Support.
  • Regions: Demacia, Noxus, Ionia, Piltover & Zaun, Freljord, Shadow Isles, Bilgewater, Shurima, Targon, Bandle City, Ixtal and the Void.
  • Custom lists: build your own list from the full champion roster and share it with friends.

Challenge modes

Classic
Work through a list alphabetically, completing champions in any order you like.
Random
The same list in a shuffled order that stays fixed until you reset it.
Hard
Only the next champion can be completed. A loss sends you back one champion: you have to beat the previous one again before trying the one you lost with.
Random + Hard
A shuffled order combined with the go-back-one-champion rule.
Insanity
Only the next champion can be completed, and a loss sends you back to the start of the run.
Random + Insanity
A shuffled order combined with the restart-on-loss rule.

How progress works

Pick a list, win a game with the champion shown as Next up, and mark it complete. Each champion keeps your attempts, completion date and an optional note. Progress bars and a statistics page (win rate, average attempts, pace and estimated finish) show how you are doing, and you can share a read-only view of your progress with a link. Role lists update as new champions are released.

Where the champion data comes from

Champion roles come from the community-maintained lolstaticdata project and are refreshed automatically each day, so newly released champions appear without anyone editing a list. Champion icons and classes (Fighter, Mage and so on) come from Riot\u2019s Data Dragon. Races and regions are lists maintained by hand, and a few champions sit in more than one on purpose. Builds and statistics under Next up are collected weekly from OP.GG.

Not the official League of Legends client

LoLphabet is an independent fan project. It is not the League of Legends client, it does not connect to your Riot account or match history, and you record your own wins. It is not endorsed by Riot Games.
